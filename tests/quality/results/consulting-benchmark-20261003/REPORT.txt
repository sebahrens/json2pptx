COVERAGE
Two coherent, fictional 12-slide decision cases were attempted on all ten
production templates: midnight-blue, p-style, modern-yellow, modern-template,
business-template, forest-green, warm-coral, blue-corporate, abstract and modern.

Quantitative case: 10 complete decks, 120 individual slide PNGs inspected.
All ten omit the default source on native table slide 5. Modern also has a
false body-role readability warning; the 10.08pt KPI caption meets its 10pt floor.
The other nine are deterministic-ready despite the table-source omission.

Strategy case: 9 complete decks, 108 individual slide PNGs inspected. Six have
material screenshot crop on slide 9. Midnight Blue, Forest Green and Warm Coral
preserve the full dashboard and pass the final visual review. Modern still
refuses generation after three template-specific repair passes; no visual
review is claimed for that ungenerated deck. Supported process overrides do
not select the advertised alternative. The initial contact-sheet review was
subsequently completed with every individual PNG; the correction is recorded.

Portability: six-slide case on all four fixtures (4x3, 21x9, side-logo,
two-master), native-master controls, attribution and presentation-scale controls.
53 individual images inspected across 13 artifacts, including 18 revised images
of already covered slots. Source-complete final wide fixtures render; the 4x3
closing remains truthfully refused after the bounded repairs. No new fitting
bug is claimed for this known capacity constraint.

Supplemental exhibits: six images across p-style, midnight-blue, modern-template.
Quote/stat/action passes; stacked chart plus supporting table compresses the
plot and is correctly flagged. Two fresh horizontal-negative controls reproduce
label collision; one explicit-source table control confirms the workaround.
These streams preserve 290 reviewed PNGs before additional historical evidence;
228 are the final production-deck slides. Counts exclude contacts and research
pages, and do not count ungenerated slides as reviewed.

REFERENCE STANDARD
18 pages visually inspected across four primary consulting publications: BCG
True-Luxury 2025, Bain Global PE 2025, PwC CEO Survey 2026, and BCG's public Dallas
client recommendations (2016). research/consulting-benchmark.json and
source-page-index.json retain direct URLs, dates, exact pages and observations.
IBCS guidance also informed message-first exhibits, direct labels, explicit
units/periods and consistent scales for comparable measures (https://www.ibcs.com/IBCS/).

Applied criteria: conclusion-led narrative; evidence linked to implications;
reconciling financial arithmetic; visible sources/units/time periods; common
scales only for comparable quantities; actual/forecast distinctions; legible
hierarchy and chart labels; intact screenshot evidence; owned, dated decisions.
The benchmark distinguishes dense read-ahead reports from live presentations.
The baseline comfortable-scale decks are not certified for room projection.
Original synthetic data and an original dashboard were used. Copyrighted
reference PDFs and their screenshots are not redistributed in this package.
